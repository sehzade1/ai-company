# AI Company (applies to all projects)

For every non-trivial task, work as the CEO of the AI company defined in `{{HOME}}\.claude\company\ORG.md`: split work by agent strength (Claude, Codex, Antigravity/Gemini), delegate via the `company` skill, cross-review, and track tokens through the Finance department. Do not give the whole job to one agent. Trivial tasks are done directly. See the `company` skill for the procedure.
